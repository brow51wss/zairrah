04 Elemental Language
The phoenix carries four elements. Each one governs a specific design decision, so the identity stays coherent as it stretches across web, print, and packaging.
Fire White flame
The phoenix wing itself. Champagne gold, diamond shimmer, one point of light per composition — the glow moment the brand is built on.
Air Soft gradient
Wide letterspacing, generous margins, gradients that fade rather than stop. Air is what the brand does with empty space — it lets the work breathe.
Water Pearl & silver
Fluid curves, reflective surfaces, pearl shimmer. Governs the curve of every rounded form and the soft reflection under product imagery.
Earth Rose gold
The wordmark’s rose-to-espresso gradient. Grids, rules, and square corners — the structural layer that keeps the softness from drifting.
05 Identity
Primary lockup — horizontal Minimum width 180px
Reversed — ink ground
Wing stays gold. Wordmark becomes bone. Never invert the wing to white.
Phoenix mark — standalone
Favicon, social avatar, packaging seal, watermark on imagery at 12% opacity.
Wordmark only
Where the mark would compete — narrow site headers, email signatures, invoice headers.
Stacked lockup
Square formats: signage, Instagram profile, packaging face. Mark above, wordmark centered beneath.
Clear space
Minimum clear space on all sides equals the cap height of the Z. Nothing crosses it — not photography, not a button, not a page edge.
Name in text
Set as ZAIRRAH in all caps. The spaced “Z a i R r a h” treatment from the old site is retired, and the mid-word capital R with it.
Never
Do not add drop shadows, outline the letterforms, stretch the lockup, recolor the wing, place the logo over a busy photograph, or separate the wing and wordmark within the same composition.

Proportion
Roughly 80% porcelain, 15% espresso ink, 5% metallics. Gold and rose gold are accents — a hairline, a number, a hover state. They are never a background fill for large areas.
Legibility
Body copy is Espresso Ink (#2D2523) or Slate (#5C554F) on Porcelain. Stone and Champagne Gold are surface and accent colors — neither passes 4.5:1 as small text. For secondary captions use #6B635C.
Retired
The current site’s slate navy (#31324B) is discontinued. Where a dark surface is needed, use Espresso Ink.

09 Voice
Calm, precise, unhurried.
Short declarative lines. Concrete nouns over adjectives. The work is described, not sold. No exclamation marks, no urgency tactics, no emoji.
Service copy pattern
Name, then three qualities. “Bridal Beauty — timeless, balanced, long-wear elegance.”
We say Clean, sculpted, camera-ready.
We don’t say Get that flawless glam look you’ve been dreaming of!
We say Lashes that open the eyes.
We don’t say Dramatic volume lashes for the ultimate wow factor.

11 Website Redesign
How the identity translates to zairrah.com. These are the decisions the rebuild inherits from this guide.
Structure Six service pages, each with its own hero and gallery. One portfolio, filtered by service — not a single 52-image wall.
Hero One full-bleed portrait, the lockup, one line of copy, one button. Nothing repeats four times.
Booking A real booking flow with service, duration, and price. The current “Book Now” links back to the homepage.
Motion Fades and slow reveals only, 400–600ms. Nothing bounces, slides fast, or auto-plays.
Surfaces Porcelain throughout. Espresso ink for the footer and one interruption section. Metallics only as hairlines and hover states.
Mobile Designed mobile-first. Most clients arrive from Instagram. Tap targets 44px minimum, one column, persistent book button.
